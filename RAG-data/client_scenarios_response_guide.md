# Client Scenarios and Response Guide

## Handling Initial Contact Situations

### Emergency and Time-Sensitive Situations

**Scenario: Client says they have a court date tomorrow**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Immediately escalate to attorney
2. Gather essential information:
   - Court name and location
   - Case number
   - What type of hearing
   - Current representation status
   - What documents they've received
3. Advise: "This is urgent. Let me connect you with an attorney immediately."
4. If after hours: Provide emergency contact number
5. Document all details

**Example Response:** "I understand you have a court appearance tomorrow. This requires immediate attention from an attorney. Let me gather some quick details and connect you right away. Can you tell me what court you need to appear in and what the hearing is for?"

---

**Scenario: Client received lawsuit papers today**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Calm the client - explain they have time to respond
2. Collect information:
   - Date served
   - Who is suing them
   - What court (state/federal)
   - Brief description of claims
   - Response deadline (usually on summons)
3. Advise typical response time: 20-30 days
4. Schedule urgent consultation
5. Advise them to not ignore it

**Example Response:** "I understand you've just been served with a lawsuit. While this is serious, you do have time to respond - typically 20 to 30 days. Let me gather some information and schedule you for a consultation within the next few days. Can you tell me who is suing you and what the lawsuit is about?"

---

**Scenario: Client arrested, calling from jail**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Get their name and booking number
2. Determine charges if known
3. Ask about arraignment date/time
4. Collect bail information
5. Note: Calls may be recorded
6. Connect to criminal defense attorney immediately
7. If attorney unavailable, take detailed message with callback priority

**Example Response:** "I can help you reach an attorney. For your protection, please know this call may be recorded. Let me get your name, booking number, and the charges if you know them. I'll connect you with a criminal defense attorney right away."

---

**Scenario: Client facing eviction**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Determine timeline: Notice received? Court date set?
2. Collect information:
   - What notices received
   - Reason for eviction
   - Court date if applicable
   - Current rent status
   - Lease terms
3. Explain urgency level
4. Schedule consultation appropriate to timeline
5. Advise them to respond to any court papers

**Example Response:** "Let me help you. First, have you received a court summons or just an eviction notice? This will determine how quickly we need to act. Generally, you have time to respond and meet with an attorney. Let's schedule a consultation and review your options."

### Financial Hardship Situations

**Scenario: Client says they can't afford attorney fees**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Don't make assumptions - discuss fee options
2. Explain various fee structures:
   - Contingency (if applicable to case type)
   - Payment plans
   - Flat fees vs. hourly
3. Mention consultation is first step
4. Provide information about:
   - Legal aid organizations (if they might qualify)
   - Pro bono services
   - Law school clinics
   - Limited scope representation options

**Example Response:** "I understand legal fees can be concerning. Our firm offers various payment options including payment plans for some matters. Some cases can be handled on contingency, meaning you only pay if we win. The consultation fee is [amount], and during that meeting the attorney can discuss all payment options specific to your situation. Would you like to schedule a consultation to explore your options?"

---

**Scenario: Client wants free advice**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Politely explain consultation fee structure
2. Clarify what consultation includes
3. Explain value of consultation
4. Mention initial consultation fee often credited if retained
5. Provide limited general information without legal advice
6. Offer referrals to free services if appropriate

**Example Response:** "I understand you're looking for information about your legal issue. While I can provide some general information, I cannot give legal advice specific to your situation. Our consultations are [fee amount] for [duration], during which an attorney will review your specific circumstances and advise you on options. If you decide to retain us, this fee is credited toward your representation. Would you like to schedule a consultation?"

### Difficult or Emotional Situations

**Scenario: Client is very upset or crying**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Show empathy and compassion
2. Speak calmly and slowly
3. Allow them to express emotions
4. Acknowledge their feelings
5. Gently gather necessary information
6. Focus on how you can help
7. Don't rush them
8. Offer tissue if in person

**Example Response:** "I can hear this is very difficult for you, and I'm sorry you're going through this. Take your time. We're here to help. When you're ready, let's talk about what's happening so we can connect you with the right attorney who can assist you."

---

**Scenario: Client is angry or confrontational**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Stay calm and professional
2. Don't take it personally
3. Listen actively
4. Acknowledge their frustration
5. Avoid being defensive
6. Set boundaries if behavior becomes abusive
7. Redirect to solution-focused conversation
8. Escalate if necessary

**Example Response:** "I understand you're frustrated with this situation. I want to help you. Let's focus on what we can do to address your legal issue. If you can share the details with me, I'll make sure you're connected with an attorney who can help resolve this."

**If abusive:** "I want to help you, but I need you to speak respectfully. If you continue to [yell/use profanity/threaten], I won't be able to assist you. Can we continue professionally so I can help?"

---

**Scenario: Client demands to speak to specific attorney immediately**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Check attorney's availability
2. If available: Transfer or take message for quick callback
3. If unavailable: Explain situation professionally
4. Offer to take detailed message
5. Provide realistic callback timeframe
6. Offer to connect with another attorney if urgent
7. Get contact information

**Example Response:** "[Attorney name] is currently [in a meeting/in court/with a client]. I'd be happy to take a detailed message and ensure they receive it. They typically return calls within [timeframe]. Is this regarding an urgent matter? If so, another attorney may be available to help you right away."

### Case Assessment Scenarios

**Scenario: Client asks if they have a case**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Explain you cannot provide legal advice
2. Gather basic facts
3. Explain consultation process
4. Note that attorney will assess during consultation
5. Don't make promises about outcomes
6. Schedule consultation if they're interested

**Example Response:** "Based on what you're telling me, this sounds like something an attorney should review. I can't assess whether you have a case, but I can schedule you for a consultation where an attorney will review the facts and advise you. During that meeting, they'll explain your options and likelihood of success. Would you like to schedule that consultation?"

---

**Scenario: Client describes situation that's not a legal matter**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Listen respectfully
2. Explain this doesn't appear to be a legal issue
3. Suggest appropriate resources if possible
4. Be diplomatic and kind
5. Offer consultation if they insist (attorney can confirm)

**Example Response:** "From what you're describing, this sounds like it might be more of a [personal/business/relationship] matter rather than a legal issue. You might benefit from [appropriate resource]. However, if you'd like an attorney to review the situation to be certain, we can schedule a consultation. Would that be helpful?"

---

**Scenario: Client's case is outside firm's practice areas**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Thank them for contacting the firm
2. Clearly explain firm doesn't handle this type of case
3. Provide referrals if possible:
   - Specific attorney recommendations
   - Bar association referral service
   - Legal aid organizations
4. Wish them well

**Example Response:** "Thank you for contacting our firm. We specialize in [firm's practice areas], and unfortunately, we don't handle [their type of case]. However, I can provide you with some referrals to attorneys who do practice in this area. [Provide names or refer to bar association.] I hope you're able to find the help you need."

### Conflict of Interest Scenarios

**Scenario: During intake, potential conflict identified**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Stop gathering information immediately
2. Explain a potential conflict may exist
3. Don't discuss details of conflict
4. Transfer to conflicts coordinator
5. Do not schedule consultation until cleared
6. Apologize for inconvenience

**Example Response:** "Based on what you've mentioned, there may be a conflict of interest that would prevent us from representing you in this matter. I need to transfer you to our conflicts coordinator who can review this. Please hold for a moment."

---

**Scenario: Client wants firm to sue current client**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Recognize conflict immediately
2. Politely decline
3. Don't discuss current client
4. Provide referrals to other attorneys
5. Document the inquiry

**Example Response:** "I appreciate you thinking of our firm, but we're unable to take cases against our current clients due to conflict of interest rules. Let me provide you with referrals to other qualified attorneys who can help you."

### Scope and Expectations Scenarios

**Scenario: Client wants guarantee of specific outcome**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Never guarantee outcomes
2. Explain no attorney can guarantee results
3. Note that's unethical
4. Explain attorney will assess likelihood during consultation
5. Describe firm's experience and track record (generally)

**Example Response:** "I understand you're looking for assurance about the outcome. While our attorneys have extensive experience with these matters and will work diligently on your behalf, no attorney can guarantee specific results. During your consultation, the attorney can discuss the strengths and weaknesses of your case and provide their professional assessment. Would you like to schedule that consultation?"

---

**Scenario: Client expects immediate resolution**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Set realistic expectations
2. Explain legal process takes time
3. Provide general timeline for their type of matter
4. Emphasize importance of doing it right vs. fast
5. Explain consultation will address timeline

**Example Response:** "I understand you'd like this resolved quickly. Legal matters typically take time due to court schedules, legal procedures, and the need to build a strong case. [Type of case] generally takes [timeframe]. During your consultation, the attorney can give you a more specific timeline for your situation. The focus is always on achieving the best outcome, which sometimes requires patience."

---

**Scenario: Client wants attorney to do something unethical or illegal**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Politely decline
2. Don't lecture or judge
3. Explain attorneys are bound by ethics rules
4. Offer to discuss legal alternatives
5. Escalate if persistent or threatening

**Example Response:** "I understand your frustration, but attorneys are bound by professional ethics rules and cannot [describe unethical act]. However, there may be legal ways to address your concerns. I'd recommend discussing this during a consultation where the attorney can explore all lawful options available to you."

### Information Gathering Scenarios

**Scenario: Client provides excessive detail/rambling**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Listen patiently initially
2. Politely redirect to key facts
3. Ask specific questions
4. Summarize what you've understood
5. Explain detailed discussion will occur during consultation

**Example Response:** "I want to make sure I understand the key points. So you're saying [summarize briefly]. Those details are important, and you'll want to share all of that with the attorney during your consultation. For now, let me get some basic information so we can schedule you with the right attorney."

---

**Scenario: Client refuses to provide necessary information**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Explain why information is needed
2. Assure confidentiality
3. Explain without information, you can't help effectively
4. Be patient but firm
5. If they won't provide info: Offer to call back when ready

**Example Response:** "I understand you may be hesitant to share details, but to connect you with the right attorney and ensure we don't have conflicts of interest, I need some basic information. Everything you tell me is confidential. The attorney will need even more details during your consultation to properly advise you. Can you share [specific information needed]?"

---

**Scenario: Client doesn't know details of their situation**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Be patient and understanding
2. Ask what they do know
3. Help them identify what documents they have
4. Advise them what to gather before consultation
5. Schedule consultation anyway if appropriate

**Example Response:** "That's okay. Many people don't have all the details at first. Let's start with what you do know. Do you have any documents related to this? [List relevant documents]. Try to gather those before your consultation - the attorney will help you understand everything. Let's go ahead and schedule your consultation."

### Special Population Scenarios

**Scenario: Elderly client who is confused**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Be patient and speak clearly
2. Don't shout (unless hearing impaired)
3. Repeat information as needed
4. Use simple language
5. Ask if family member or caregiver should be included
6. Consider in-person consultation if they have difficulty
7. Check for signs of undue influence or exploitation

**Example Response:** "Let me make sure I understand. You need help with [repeat back simply]. Is there a family member or trusted friend who helps you with important matters? They're welcome to come to the consultation with you. Would that be helpful?"

---

**Scenario: Non-English speaking client**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Determine their preferred language
2. Use interpreter service if available
3. Speak clearly and use simple English if minimal proficiency
4. Check if firm has bilingual attorneys
5. Arrange for interpreter for consultation
6. Be patient and respectful

**Example Response:** "I want to make sure I understand you correctly and that you understand me. What language are you most comfortable speaking? [Arrange for interpreter.] We want to ensure you receive the best service, so we'll have an interpreter available for your consultation."

---

**Scenario: Client with disability**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Ask if they need accommodations
2. Offer appropriate assistance
3. For physical disability: Ensure accessible meeting location
4. For hearing impairment: Offer video relay, in-person with interpreter, written communication
5. For vision impairment: Offer to read documents, provide large print
6. Treat with respect and dignity

**Example Response:** "We want to ensure you're comfortable and can fully participate in the consultation. Do you need any accommodations? [After they specify:] Absolutely, we can arrange that. Let me make a note to ensure everything is ready for your consultation."

### Follow-Up Scenarios

**Scenario: Client calling for status update**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Verify identity
2. Check case management system
3. Provide available information
4. If no update: Explain typical timeline
5. Offer to have attorney call if complex
6. Note the inquiry in system

**Example Response:** "Let me pull up your file. [Review.] I can see that [provide status update]. The attorney is [working on X/waiting for Y]. If you have specific questions, I can have the attorney call you back. Would you like me to arrange that?"

---

**Scenario: Client hasn't heard from attorney in weeks**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Apologize for concern
2. Check file for recent activity
3. Explain what's been happening (if visible in system)
4. Acknowledge their frustration
5. Promise to have attorney call by specific time
6. Follow up to ensure callback happens

**Example Response:** "I understand your concern about not hearing from us. Let me check your file. [Review.] I can see [attorney action]. I apologize if that wasn't communicated to you. I'm going to send a message to [attorney] right now to call you by end of day tomorrow. Can I confirm your best contact number?"

### Billing Scenarios

**Scenario: Client disputes bill**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Listen to their concerns
2. Don't be defensive
3. Get specific details about disputed items
4. Transfer to billing department or attorney
5. Assure concerns will be addressed
6. Document the dispute

**Example Response:** "I understand you have concerns about your invoice. Let me get some details about what specifically you're questioning, and I'll make sure the right person reviews this with you. Can you tell me which charges you're concerned about?"

---

**Scenario: Client can't pay bill**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Express understanding
2. Transfer to billing department
3. Don't make payment arrangements yourself
4. Note that payment plans may be available
5. Be compassionate but professional

**Example Response:** "I understand financial situations can change. Let me connect you with our billing department. They can discuss payment options with you. Please hold while I transfer you."

### Ending Contact Scenarios

**Scenario: Client wants to terminate representation**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Don't argue or take personally
2. Ask if they'd like to discuss concerns with attorney first
3. Explain termination process
4. Note any pending deadlines or court dates
5. Transfer to attorney immediately
6. Document request

**Example Response:** "I understand you'd like to end your representation with us. Before we process that, would you like to speak with your attorney about your concerns? There may be issues that can be resolved. Also, I want to make sure you're aware of [any pending deadlines]. Let me connect you with [attorney] right away."

---

**Scenario: Closing out matter - case resolved**

**Response Protocol:**
1. Congratulate if appropriate
2. Explain final steps
3. Discuss final billing
4. Explain file retention policy
5. Provide information about requesting files later
6. Thank them for choosing the firm
7. Invite them to contact for future needs

**Example Response:** "Congratulations on the resolution of your case! I'll make sure the attorney sends you copies of all final documents. You should receive your final invoice within [timeframe]. We'll retain your file for [retention period], and you can always request copies. Thank you for trusting us with your legal matter. Please don't hesitate to contact us if you need legal assistance in the future."

## General Best Practices

### Always Do:
- Listen actively and empathetically
- Take detailed notes
- Verify and repeat back important information
- Set clear expectations
- Follow up as promised
- Maintain confidentiality
- Be professional and courteous
- Stay calm under pressure
- Escalate when appropriate
- Document all interactions

### Never Do:
- Give legal advice
- Guarantee outcomes
- Make assumptions
- Discuss other clients
- Share personal opinions about legal matters
- Promise things you can't deliver
- Argue with clients
- Ignore urgent matters
- Discuss fees you're not authorized to discuss
- Dismiss client concerns

### Key Phrases to Use:
- "I understand..."
- "Let me make sure I have this right..."
- "That sounds like something an attorney should review..."
- "I'll make sure [attorney] receives this information..."
- "For your protection..."
- "Let me connect you with someone who can help..."
- "Thank you for your patience..."
- "I appreciate you sharing that with me..."

### Red Flag Situations to Escalate Immediately:
- Emergency situations or imminent harm
- Threats of violence or suicide
- Potential conflicts of interest
- Requests for unethical actions
- Abuse of attorney or staff
- Highly complex matters beyond your understanding
- Cases requiring immediate attorney attention
- Media inquiries about clients or cases

---

*This guide provides general approaches to common scenarios. Use professional judgment and consult with attorneys when uncertain. Client service is paramount - when in doubt, escalate to ensure clients receive appropriate assistance.*
