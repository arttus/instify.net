# Document Requirements by Matter Type

## Introduction

This guide provides comprehensive checklists of documents clients should gather for their initial consultation and throughout representation. Having these documents ready helps attorneys provide accurate advice and efficient service.

---

## Family Law

### Divorce - General Documents

**Personal Identification**
- Driver's license or government-issued photo ID
- Social Security cards for both spouses and all children
- Birth certificates for all children
- Marriage certificate
- Prenuptial or postnuptial agreement (if applicable)

**Financial Documents - Assets**
- Last 3 years of tax returns (federal and state)
- Last 3 months of pay stubs for both spouses
- Last 3 months of bank statements (all accounts - checking, savings, money market)
- Investment account statements (stocks, bonds, mutual funds, 401k, IRA, pensions)
- Retirement account statements (all accounts)
- Business financial statements (if either spouse owns business)
- Real estate deeds and mortgage statements
- Vehicle titles and loan statements
- Life insurance policies
- Credit card statements (all cards, last 3 months)
- Personal loan documents
- List of personal property (furniture, jewelry, artwork, collections)
- Cryptocurrency account statements

**Financial Documents - Debts**
- Credit card statements showing balances
- Loan documents (auto, personal, student, business)
- Mortgage statements
- Home equity line documents
- Medical bills
- Tax debt documentation
- Any other outstanding debts

**Income Documentation**
- W-2 forms (last 3 years)
- 1099 forms (last 3 years)
- Bonuses and commission statements
- Rental income documentation
- Social Security or disability statements
- Unemployment benefits documentation
- Pension or retirement income statements

**Expenses Documentation**
- Monthly budget worksheet
- Childcare expenses
- Medical and dental expenses
- School tuition and expenses
- Mortgage or rent payments
- Utility bills
- Insurance premiums (health, auto, home, life)
- Vehicle expenses
- Child activity expenses

**Marital History**
- Timeline of separation
- Timeline of significant marital events
- Documentation of prior separations or reconciliations
- Prior court orders or legal agreements

**Child-Related Documents** (if applicable)
- School records
- Medical records
- Extracurricular activity schedules and costs
- Childcare provider information
- Special needs documentation
- Current custody arrangements
- Calendar showing current parenting time

### Child Custody

**Child Information**
- Birth certificates
- Social Security cards
- Medical records and immunization records
- School records and report cards
- Special needs or disability documentation
- Therapy or counseling records
- Extracurricular activity information

**Parent Information**
- Work schedules
- Home environment photos
- Character references
- Criminal background (if relevant)
- Substance abuse evaluation/treatment records (if applicable)
- Mental health records (if relevant)
- Domestic violence records (if applicable)

**Current Arrangements**
- Existing custody orders
- Parenting plans
- Calendar showing actual parenting time
- Communication logs with other parent
- Child support payment records

**Documentation of Concerns**
- Incident reports (dates, times, witnesses)
- Police reports
- Protective orders
- Photos or videos showing concerns
- Medical records showing injuries
- School attendance records
- Text messages, emails (demonstrating parenting issues)

### Child Support

**Income Documentation**
- Last 2 years of tax returns
- Last 3 months of pay stubs
- Year-to-date earnings statement
- Bonus and commission documentation
- Self-employment income records
- Rental income documentation
- Investment income statements
- Unemployment or disability benefits
- Social Security statements
- Workers' compensation benefits

**Child Expenses**
- Health insurance premiums for children
- Unreimbursed medical expenses
- Childcare costs
- School expenses (tuition, fees, supplies)
- Extracurricular activities
- Special needs expenses
- Transportation costs

**Existing Orders**
- Current child support order
- Payment history
- Arrears documentation
- Modification history

### Adoption

**Prospective Parents**
- Birth certificates
- Marriage certificate (if applicable)
- Divorce decrees (if applicable)
- Proof of income (tax returns, pay stubs)
- Employment verification
- Financial statements
- Medical clearances
- Home study report
- Background checks
- References

**Child Information**
- Birth certificate
- Medical records
- Social Security card
- Adoption placement documentation
- Birth parent consents (if applicable)
- Termination of parental rights orders (if applicable)

## Personal Injury

### Motor Vehicle Accident

**Accident Information**
- Police report
- Accident report number
- Photos of accident scene
- Photos of vehicle damage
- Photos of injuries
- Witness names and contact information
- Diagram of accident

**Insurance Information**
- Your insurance policy and declarations page
- Other driver's insurance information
- Correspondence from insurance companies
- Claim numbers

**Medical Documentation**
- Emergency room records
- Ambulance records
- Hospital records
- Doctor's notes and records
- Physical therapy records
- Prescription records
- Medical bills (all providers)
- Medical imaging (X-rays, MRIs, CT scans)
- Future treatment plans
- Medical devices or equipment costs

**Lost Wages**
- Pay stubs before and after accident
- Letter from employer about missed work
- Tax returns if self-employed
- Documentation of lost opportunities
- Proof of sick time or vacation used

**Other Damages**
- Property damage estimates and receipts
- Rental car receipts
- Out-of-pocket expenses
- Receipts for assistance needed (house cleaning, childcare, etc.)

**Correspondence**
- All letters from insurance companies
- All emails or messages about the accident
- Settlement offers
- Recorded statements (do not give without attorney)

### Slip and Fall / Premises Liability

**Incident Documentation**
- Incident report from property owner
- Photos of dangerous condition
- Photos of injuries
- Witness information
- Video footage (if available)
- Documentation of complaints about condition

**Medical Documentation**
- Same as motor vehicle accident section

**Property Information**
- Property owner information
- Property manager information
- Maintenance records (if obtainable)
- History of similar incidents (if known)

### Medical Malpractice

**Medical Records**
- Complete medical records from treating provider
- Records from other providers (before and after)
- Lab results and test results
- Surgical records
- Medication records
- Medical bills

**Timeline**
- Chronology of treatment
- When problem discovered
- Symptoms and progression

**Expert Information**
- Names of potential expert witnesses
- Second opinion documentation
- Independent medical examination results

**Communication**
- Correspondence with medical providers
- Documentation of complaints
- Hospital patient relations records

### Product Liability

**Product Information**
- Photos of product
- Product packaging and labels
- Receipts or proof of purchase
- Serial numbers or model numbers
- Instructions or warnings
- Product registration

**Incident Documentation**
- Photos of defect
- Photos of injury or damage
- Witness information
- Similar incidents (if known)

**Medical Documentation**
- Injury treatment records
- Medical bills

## Real Estate

### Residential Purchase

**Financial Documents**
- Pre-approval letter from lender
- Bank statements (2-3 months)
- Pay stubs (last 2 months)
- Tax returns (last 2 years)
- W-2s (last 2 years)
- Employment verification letter
- Gift letter (if down payment is gifted)
- Bankruptcy or foreclosure documentation (if applicable)

**Property Documents**
- Purchase agreement
- Property listing information
- Home inspection report
- Appraisal (when available)
- Homeowners insurance quote
- Homeowners association documents
- Property disclosure statements

**Transaction Documents**
- Title commitment
- Survey
- Closing disclosure (preliminary)
- Loan estimate

### Residential Sale

**Property Documents**
- Deed
- Mortgage payoff statement
- Second mortgage or HELOC information
- Property tax bills
- Homeowners association documents
- Home warranty information
- Recent improvements and receipts
- Appliance warranties and manuals

**Disclosure Documents**
- Property disclosure statement
- Lead paint disclosure (if pre-1978)
- HOA financial documents
- Covenants, conditions, restrictions (CC&Rs)

**Transaction Documents**
- Listing agreement
- Purchase agreement
- Inspection reports
- Appraisal

### Landlord-Tenant

**For Landlords (Eviction)**
- Lease agreement
- Written notices served
- Proof of service
- Payment ledger
- Photos of property damage
- Communication with tenant
- Police reports (if applicable)
- Witness statements

**For Tenants (Lease Disputes)**
- Lease agreement
- Rent payment receipts or cancelled checks
- Communication with landlord
- Photos of property condition
- Photos of issues (repairs needed, harassment, etc.)
- Repair requests
- Move-in/move-out inspection reports
- Security deposit documentation

## Employment Law

### Wrongful Termination / Discrimination

**Employment Documents**
- Offer letter
- Employment contract
- Employee handbook
- Performance reviews
- Disciplinary notices
- Termination letter or notice
- Severance agreement (do not sign without reviewing with attorney)
- Pay stubs
- W-2s

**Evidence of Discrimination/Harassment**
- Timeline of events
- Emails, text messages, voicemails
- Witness names and contact information
- Notes from meetings
- Complaints to HR or management
- HR investigation documents
- Comparative evidence (others treated differently)

**Financial Information**
- Salary and benefits information
- Bonuses and commissions
- Stock options or equity
- Unemployment benefits information
- New employment information

**Medical Documentation** (if applicable)
- Medical leave documentation
- Disability documentation
- Workers' compensation claims
- Medical records related to stress or harassment

### Wage and Hour Claims

**Time Records**
- Timesheets or time cards
- Clock-in/clock-out records
- Schedule
- Emails or texts about hours worked
- Personal calendar or notes

**Pay Documentation**
- Pay stubs (all during employment)
- Final paycheck
- Bonus information
- Commission structure and payments
- Expense reimbursement policies and claims

**Employment Documents**
- Job description
- Offer letter
- Employment contract
- Employee classification (exempt/non-exempt)
- Employee handbook

## Immigration

### Family-Based Green Card

**Petitioner (U.S. Citizen/Permanent Resident)**
- Proof of citizenship or permanent residence
- Birth certificate
- Passport
- Marriage certificate (if petitioning spouse)
- Divorce decrees from prior marriages
- Financial documents (tax returns, pay stubs, employment letter)
- Proof of domicile in U.S.

**Beneficiary (Foreign National)**
- Birth certificate
- Passport
- Marriage certificate
- Divorce decrees from prior marriages
- Police certificates from all countries lived
- Medical examination results
- Photos (passport style)
- Evidence of relationship

**Evidence of Relationship**
- Joint bank accounts
- Joint property ownership
- Joint utility bills
- Joint insurance policies
- Photos together over time
- Affidavits from family and friends
- Travel documents showing time together
- Communication records

### Employment-Based Green Card

**Employee Documents**
- Resume/CV
- Diplomas and transcripts
- Professional licenses
- Employment letters
- Publications
- Awards and recognition

**Employer Documents**
- Business license
- Tax returns
- Financial statements
- Organizational chart
- Job offer letter
- Job description
- Recruitment documentation
- Prevailing wage determination

**Personal Documents**
- Green card

- Divorce decrees

**Residence Documentation**

## Tips for Document Preparation

**Organization**
- Sort documents by category
- Put in chronological order
- Label clearly
- Make copies (keep originals safe)
- Create index or list

**Digital Documents**
- Save in organized folders
- Use clear file names
- Create backup copies
- Consider scanning paper documents

**What to Bring to Consultation**
- Bring copies, not originals (unless specifically requested)
- Bring written summary of situation
- Bring questions you want answered
- Bring calendar to schedule follow-up

**Ongoing Representation**
- Provide documents promptly when requested
- Keep attorney updated on new developments
- Save all correspondence
- Ask before discarding anything related to case
