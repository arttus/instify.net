## Challenges and Considerations

### Ethical and Professional Responsibility

**Key Concerns**:
- Maintaining attorney-client privilege and confidentiality
- Ensuring competent representation with AI assistance
- Avoiding unauthorized practice of law by AI systems
- Managing conflicts of interest in AI-shared platforms
- Maintaining professional judgment and oversight

**Best Practices**:
- Implement robust data security and privacy protections
- Maintain human oversight of all AI-generated work
- Clearly define AI capabilities and limitations
- Regularly audit AI performance and accuracy
- Stay current with evolving ethical guidelines

### Change Management

**Common Resistance Factors**:
- Fear of job displacement or skill obsolescence
- Concern about technology reliability and accuracy
- Resistance to changing established workflows
- Uncertainty about client acceptance and satisfaction
- Investment costs and ROI concerns

**Success Strategies**:
- Emphasize AI as enhancement, not replacement
- Provide comprehensive training and support
- Demonstrate clear benefits and improvements
- Start with willing early adopters
- Celebrate successes and share positive outcomes
