# Implementing AI Reception in Your Law Firm: A 7-14 Day Roadmap

Implementing new technology in a law firm can feel overwhelming, especially when it involves something as critical as client communication. But with proper planning and a structured approach, deploying an AI voice receptionist can be smooth, efficient, and transformative for your practice.

## Pre-Implementation: Setting the Foundation

### Week Before Go-Live: Preparation Phase

**Day -7 to -5: Initial Assessment**

*What you'll do:*
- Audit current call handling processes
- Document existing phone system setup
- Identify integration requirements
- Review staff roles and responsibilities

*What to prepare:*
- Current phone system details
- List of practice areas and specialties
- Existing CRM/case management systems
- Staff schedules and responsibilities
- Common client inquiry types

*Time investment:* 2-3 hours total

**Day -4 to -2: Information Gathering**

*Required information for setup:*
- Firm name and branding preferences
- Attorney names and specialties
- Office hours and holiday schedules
- Emergency contact protocols
- Preferred appointment types and durations
- Common legal terminology for your practice areas

*Integration details needed:*
- Calendar system credentials
- CRM system access
- Case management platform details
- Email system information
- Phone system configuration

*Time investment:* 1-2 hours total

**Day -1: Final Preparation**

*Staff preparation:*
- Brief team on upcoming changes
- Explain benefits and address concerns
- Review new call handling procedures
- Prepare backup protocols

*System preparation:*
- Confirm all integrations are ready
- Test phone system compatibility
- Verify calendar access
- Check CRM connections

*Time investment:* 1 hour

## Week 1: Soft Launch and Initial Deployment

### Day 1-2: System Configuration

**Day 1 Morning: Initial Setup**
- AI system configuration begins
- Practice area customization
- Basic call routing rules established
- Integration testing starts

**Day 1 Afternoon: First Tests**
- Internal test calls
- Basic functionality verification
- Integration testing
- Initial adjustments

**Day 2: Refinement**
- Conversation flow optimization
- Terminology fine-tuning
- Routing rule adjustments
- Staff training on new procedures

*Your involvement:* 30 minutes daily for feedback and approval

### Day 3-4: Limited Deployment

**Soft Launch Parameters:**
- Business hours only (no after-hours yet)
- Simple call types first
- Human backup readily available
- Extensive monitoring and logging

**What to expect:**
- 80-90% of calls handled successfully
- Some transfers to human staff
- Detailed performance reports
- Real-time adjustments

**Your role:**
- Monitor call reports
- Provide feedback on interactions
- Approve conversation improvements
- Maintain normal staff availability

*Time investment:* 15-20 minutes daily

### Day 5-7: Optimization Phase

**Performance Analysis:**

- Analyze client satisfaction feedback
- Document improvement opportunities

**System Improvements:**
- Refine conversation flows
- Add practice-specific responses
- Optimize routing decisions
- Enhance integration functionality

**Staff Adjustment:**
- Train team on new call patterns
- Adjust internal procedures
- Optimize handoff protocols
- Prepare for full deployment

*Your involvement:* 20-30 minutes daily

## Staff Training and Change Management

### Preparing Your Team

**Training Components:**

- When to expect transfers
- How to provide feedback for improvements

**Ongoing Support:**
